# Task Escalation Policy

## High-risk task criteria

- A task is high risk when it is overdue, blocked, or has a critical priority.
- A task with two or more previous delays should receive manager review even when its due date has not passed.
- The reviewer must record the reason and supporting task evidence before approving an escalation.

## Manager response

The manager should contact the responsible team, confirm the blocker, and record the next action and expected completion date. Do not mark a task complete until the operational evidence is available.
